An exposition of OpenAI’s proof of Barnette’s conjecture

This is an exposition of the proof of Barnette’s conjecture in OpenAI’s release Paired states and Hamiltonian cycles in cubic bipartite planar graphs . The algorithm is simplified and visualized with different example graphs.
